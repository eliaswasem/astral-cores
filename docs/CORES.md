# Core Abilities

## 1. Aero Core

- **Active – Tornado Lift:** You spawn a violent updraft around you, launching all enemies within a 5-block radius into the air.
    - **Cooldown:**
- **Passive – Featherweight:** You are completely immune to fall damage. When you land, white cloud particles spawn around your feet and damage nearby enemies.

## 2. Gale Core

- **Active – Sonic Dash:** You dash forward, creating a sonic boom that deals 2.5 hearts of damage and applies minor knockback.
    - **Cooldown:**
- **Passive – Lightfeet:** You permanently gain Speed I. While sprinting, wind particles appear beneath your feet.

## 3. Chrono Core

- **Active – Time Return:** You rewind your position to where you were 5 seconds ago.
    - **Cooldown:**
- **Passive – Second Timeline:** You have a 50% chance to cheat death and fully heal. This ability has a global 10-minute cooldown, whether it succeeds or fails.

## 4. Gravity Core

- **Active – Gravity Pull:** You pull nearby players toward you.
    - **Cooldown:**
- **Passive – Heavy Presence:** You permanently gain 50% knockback resistance.

## 5. Frost Core

- **Active – Frost Lock:** You freeze a player in place for 2 seconds.
    - **Cooldown:**
- **Passive – Frost Aura:** You emit a Slowness aura that becomes stronger the closer enemies are to you.

## 6. Phoenix Core

- **Active – Phoenix Burst:** You unleash a massive fiery explosion that knocks back and burns nearby enemies.
    - **Cooldown:**
- **Passive – Flameborn:** You are completely immune to fire and lava and cannot burn. While inside the Nether, you permanently gain Regeneration I.

## 7. Leviathan Core

- **Active – Whirlpool:** You create a water vortex that pulls nearby players toward its center. The vortex is twice as strong underwater.
    - **Cooldown:**
- **Passive – Oceanborn:** You gain increased swim speed and a 10-minute breath meter. While it is raining or you are in water, you gain additional stat buffs.

## 8. Shadow Core

- **Active – Smoke Veil:** You deploy an 8-second smoke cloud. Enemies inside it gain Blindness I, while you gain Speed II and +20% melee damage.
    - **Cooldown:**
- **Passive – Living Shadow:** When you sneak in darkness for 5 seconds, you become invisible. Your invisibility ends when you sprint or attack.

## 9. Berserker Core

- **Active – Rage Mode:** For 10 seconds, you gain +30% melee damage. Your natural health regeneration is disabled during this time. You also gain Strength II, Speed II, and Fire Resistance I for 1 minute and 30 seconds, and you are fully healed.
    - **Cooldown:**
- **Passive – Bloodlust:** Each kill restores 2 hearts and grants you Speed II and Strength III. If you have 3.5 hearts or less, you gain an additional Strength boost.

## 10. Illusion Core

- **Active – Mirror Swap:** You spawn a stationary mirror decoy, become invisible, and teleport 10–15 blocks away.
    - **Cooldown:**
- **Passive – Mirror Image:** When you are hit, you have a small chance to create a visual illusion that misdirects your enemy's aim.

## 11. Nature Core

- **Active – Root Trap:** You create a field of leaf particles that roots nearby players with vines. The trap has an increased radius in natural biomes.
    - **Cooldown:**
- **Passive – Nature's Blessing:** While inside a natural biome, you gain increased regeneration, minor Speed, improved food healing, and faster crop growth.

## 12. Magnet Core

- **Active – Magnetic Pull:** You instantly pull all item drops and XP orbs within a 15-block radius directly to you, vacuuming up nearby loot.
    - **Cooldown:** 15 seconds
- **Passive – Magnetic Disarm:** Your critical hits have a 50% chance to magnetize your enemy's weapon, inflicting Heavy Mining Fatigue for 1.5 seconds. This completely freezes their attack cooldown, preventing them from swinging their weapon.
