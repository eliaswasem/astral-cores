# Astral Cores

## First Age

### The First Fall

A long time ago, twelve meteors fell from the sky.

There was no warning and no explanation. People watched as they came down in different parts of the world, leaving large craters where they struck.

The meteors themselves weren't completely unknown. They were mostly made from materials people already knew, although their strange arrival made them worth exploring.

Eventually, people began exploring the impact sites.

Inside the meteors, they found something they had never seen before.

At the center of each meteor was a single object.

A Core.

Nobody knew what the Cores were or what they could do. That changed when someone discovered what happened when a Core was claimed.

They gained abilities that no ordinary person could have.

Word spread quickly, and the other meteors were explored as well. Soon, all twelve Cores had been found.

Because the objects had come from the sky, they became known as Astral Cores.

At first, little was known about them. As more people came into contact with the Cores, their abilities slowly became better understood.

### The Rise

As the knowledge surrounding the Cores spread, so did their use.

The powers of the Cores were passed from one person to another. Over time, those who possessed them learned more about their abilities and became better at using them.

Some Cores were used to protect settlements. Others were used for travel, exploration, hunting, or simply to make life easier.

The Cores became an important part of many people's lives, but they also changed the balance of power.

A person with an Astral Core could do things an ordinary soldier could never have done. A group with several Cores could become difficult to challenge.

It didn't take long for people to start arguing over who should control them.

Some believed the Cores should be shared.

Others believed they should belong to those strong enough to protect them.

There were also those who simply wanted the power for themselves.

As more people became involved, disagreements turned into conflicts, and eventually those conflicts grew into something much larger.

### The War

Nobody remembers exactly how the war started.

By the time it was over, it didn't matter anymore.

The twelve Cores were divided between different groups, and the fight for control continued for years.

People were hunted, and Cores were stolen. Some changed hands several times during the same conflict. For every Core that was taken, someone else wanted it back.

During the fighting, some people tried to hide their Cores by throwing them into the Void.

That didn't work.

After some time, another meteor would fall somewhere else, and at its center would be the same Core that had been lost.

At first, nobody understood what was happening.

Then it happened again.

And again.

Eventually, people learned that the twelve Cores could not be permanently removed this way. They could be lost or disappear for a while, but sooner or later, they would be found again.

Both sides of the war knew this.

The twelve Cores could not simply be destroyed or hidden forever. If someone wanted one badly enough, they could wait for its next meteor.

The fighting only became worse.

### The End

After years of war, there was little left of the world that had existed before it.

The remaining groups eventually realized that they were never going to gain anything by continuing. Even if one side took every Core, someone else would eventually try to take them back.

The Cores had become one of the main reasons people kept fighting.

So the remaining sides agreed on something they had never agreed on before.

The Cores had to go.

They knew what would happen if they threw them into the Void. They had seen it many times before. The twelve Cores would disappear, and eventually new meteors would fall somewhere else.

That was enough.

They didn't need to destroy them. They only needed them gone.

The twelve Astral Cores were gathered together and thrown into the Void.

Then everyone waited.

Days passed.

Then weeks.

Nothing happened.

No meteors fell, and the Cores didn't return.

At first, people assumed they simply needed more time.

Then they waited longer.

Still nothing.

For the first time since the Cores had been discovered, they were truly gone.

Nobody knew why.

The war was finally over, but the world it had left behind was very different.

Settlements had been abandoned. Old roads disappeared. Buildings fell apart. People moved away from places that had once been important, and the land slowly began to cover what had been left behind.

Years became decades.

Decades became generations.

Eventually, the people who had lived through the war were gone.

The knowledge that had been passed from one person to another disappeared with them.

There were still stories about strange powers and meteors that had once fallen from the sky, but fewer people believed them with every generation.

The old impact sites became difficult to recognize.

Some were buried.

Some were overgrown.

Some were simply forgotten.

Eventually, there was almost nothing left to connect the world to the First Age.

The Astral Cores were gone.

The people who had possessed them were gone.

And the reason they had disappeared was lost with them.
